Historical Virtools export tool

The original author recommended yyc12345's vtobjplugin for exporting a complete
Virtools file as OBJ with materials and object-placement scripts. This is a legacy
authoring reference, not a dependency of current player builds.

Project: https://github.com/yyc12345/vtobjplugin
